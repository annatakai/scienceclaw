Methodology: formulas

Returnpresent value of expected dollar loss removed / gross cost. Options on one asset are chosen by NPV = benefit - gross cost; candidates are ranked by return only when the budget binds.
Failure probabilityp(t) = min(1, C x PoF(t) / 100), with C chosen so expected city-wide breaks equal N_target (breaks per mile x network miles).
Dollars per failureF = emergency multiplier x planned pipe cost + (consequence index / 100) x worst-case social cost. Consequence excludes traffic and direct cost (no double counting).
Pipe benefitsum over the treatment life of discount(t) x [expected loss before - expected loss after] x F. Reline removes a share of age; replace resets it. Waiting a year raises cost by the inflation rate and loses one year of benefit.
Road benefitsum of discount(t) x vehicle-miles x [user cost per mile before - after]; user cost per mile = u0 x ((100 - PCR) / 100)^2.
Gross costpipe alone = dig + pipe work + repaving penalty; road alone = dig + road work; bundle = one dig + both works (no penalty). Penalty = repave cost x lost years / cycle, lost years = lambda x (cycle - street age).
Shared digA bundle wins on NPV when NPV(bundle) > NPV(pipe alone) + NPV(road alone). Each add-on must also clear the hurdle on its own work cost (marginal return).
UncertaintyReturns are recomputed with the lower and upper pipe failure bounds. A project whose low return is under the hurdle while its high return reaches it is flagged for engineering review.
Priority tiersUrgency 0.9+ is Tier 1 Urgent (funded first, up to a cap), 0.6 to 0.9 Soon, 0.3 to 0.6 Plan, below 0.3 Monitor.
FinancingEach project is paid from the cheapest source with room within its share and eligibility. Net cost = gross cost x present value of repayments per dollar (interest-free loan 0.811, state revolving loan 0.800, bonds 1.0, rate revenue 1.0). Ranking uses gross cost.
ReserveEmergency repairs are covered by a reserve sized from the 95th percentile of simulated yearly emergency repair cost, capped as a share of the budget.
