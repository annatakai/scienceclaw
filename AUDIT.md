## Other findings

- **Road urgency:** 90% of funded projects in the full run are tier 1 Urgent (1,403 of 1,570) because `urgency_from_pcr` is 1.0 at PCR 40 or below and many simulated roads sit at PCR 0. The simulated pavement model deteriorates to "failed" quickly, so tier colours carry little information until roads are calibrated.
- **Slack budget:** capital is hurdle-limited, not money-limited (isolated spends $60M of about $300M spendable over ten years on the full network). The headline coordination result depends on this regime.
- **Central beats coordinated** at every scale tried, so no claim against a central planner.
- **Results files** are under `outputs/` (git-ignored except `outputs/cip/` and the reserve cache); rerun the commands above to regenerate.
